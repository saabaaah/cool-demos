# Territory Wars

**Strategic Territorial Conquest Simulation**

Watch autonomous agents battle for territorial control in this beautiful, configurable simulation. Features multiple themes, particle effects, smooth animations, and strategic gameplay mechanics.

![Territory Wars](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Active-success)
![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-blue)

## Features

- 🎨 **Multiple Themes**: Day vs Night, Good vs Evil, 4 Seasons, Condiments War
- ⚡ **Configurable Parameters**: Adjust agent count, speed, and theme
- 🎯 **Strategic Targeting**: Seasons theme features intelligent border targeting
- ✨ **Particle Effects**: Theme-specific visual effects (sparkles, fire, snow, flowers, splashes)
- 🎮 **Fully Automatic**: Watch the simulation unfold without intervention
- 📱 **Responsive Design**: Works on desktop and mobile devices

## Quick Start

### Development

```sh
npx serve
```

Open the link in your browser and you're ready to start!

### Usage

1. Click "Start Simulation" on the landing page
2. Configure your simulation:
   - **Agents Per Side**: Number of agents per team (default: 1)
   - **Speed**: Simulation speed (Very Slow to Very Fast)
   - **Theme**: Choose from 4 unique themes
3. Click "Start Simulation" to begin
4. Watch the territorial battle unfold!

## Themes

### Day vs Night
The classic battle between light and dark with soft particle effects.

### Good vs Evil
A dramatic confrontation with sparkles for good and fire particles for evil.

### 4 Seasons
Four teams representing Spring, Summer, Fall, and Winter with seasonal effects:
- Spring: Flower particles 🌸
- Summer: Sun rays ☀️
- Fall: Falling leaves 🍂
- Winter: Snowflakes ❄️

**Special Feature**: Each season's agents intelligently target the border to the next season, creating a circular flow pattern.

### Condiments War
A playful 3-team battle between Ketchup, Mayo, and Mustard with splash effects.

## Technical Details

- **Pure JavaScript**: No frameworks, vanilla JS for maximum performance
- **Canvas-based Rendering**: Smooth 60fps animations
- **Modular Architecture**: Easy to extend with new themes
- **Theme System**: Each theme has unique visual styles and particle effects
